# Version 3.0 — Lead Attribution & Automation Layer

- Added Lead Attribution & Automation workspace.
- Added editable lead register with project, source, campaign, stage, quality, date and estimated opportunity value.
- Added source-level qualification comparisons and rule-based growth signals.
- Added on-demand weekly summary generation and CSV export for lead attribution.
- Added a Windows launcher that selects a free local port between 8000 and 8010 to reduce port-conflict friction.
- Kept all sample data local to the browser; no live ad-platform, CRM or sales-system integrations are enabled.
